Excellence in beauty. Built on relationships.

KMR Global is a UK-based wholesale distributor of premium beauty and personal care brands. We exist to connect brands your customers love with retailers who know how to sell them.

Our journey

From small distributor to trusted partner.

KMR Global was founded with a simple conviction: the UK beauty market deserves a wholesale partner that treats every retailer — from single-store boutiques to national chains — with the same care and expertise.

We started small, focused on quality and service. Over the years we've broadened our brand partnerships, expanded our warehouse footprint in Watford, and built a team of category specialists who know beauty inside out.

Today, we're the trusted distribution partner for retailers across the UK and Europe — and for a growing number of premium brands looking for a serious wholesale presence in the region.
