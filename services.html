Common questions

Everything you need to know.

What products do you offer?

We specialise in wholesale distribution of skincare, makeup, haircare, fragrance, bodycare and men's grooming products across a curated portfolio of premium and prestige brands. Contact us for our full current stocklist.

How do I place an order?

Once you have a trade account, you can place orders directly via email, phone, or through your account manager. First-time enquiries and quotes can be sent through our contact form.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes — minimums vary by brand and category. They're designed to be workable for boutique retailers as well as larger chains. Your account manager will walk you through the tiers.

What is your shipping policy?

We ship across the UK with 2–3 working-day standard delivery once your order is confirmed. Palletised and next-day options available on request. European shipping is quoted per order.

Can I return products?

Yes — unopened, unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Please contact your account manager to initiate a return and we'll walk you through the process.

Do you provide samples?

Yes, samples are available on request for evaluated new lines. Speak to your account manager or send us a note via the contact form.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes — trade pricing is tiered by volume. Larger and repeat orders unlock better pricing automatically. No haggling required.

How can I contact support?

Reach us via the contact form, at info@kmrglobal.co.uk, or on +44 20 3411 2151 during business hours. We respond to trade enquiries within one working day.
