Our process

From first conversation to first shipment.

Introduction

Send us a brief on your brand, target categories, and UK ambition. Our team reviews and comes back within a working week.

Assessment

A working session on market fit, retail strategy, and commercial expectations. We're straight about what we can deliver.

Agreement

Terms confirmed — exclusive distribution or wholesale — plus launch plan and first-year commercial targets.

Launch

Onboarding, warehousing setup, retailer conversations begin, marketing plan activates. First orders typically within 60–90 days.
